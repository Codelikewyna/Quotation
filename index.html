Executive Summary

CodeLikeWyna Tech is pleased to present this proposal for the design, development, testing, and deployment of the Gold Link digital marketplace platform.

Gold Link is intended to become a trusted digital marketplace where verified gold vendors can list physical gold for sale and buyers can securely discover and purchase gold through a structured digital platform.

The proposed solution will include, depending on the selected package:

Marketing Website Marketplace Platform Android Application iOS Application Administration Dashboard Payment Integration KYC / Identity Verification Gold Price Display Transaction Management

The project will follow a phased development approach, beginning with the public-facing website and progressing into the marketplace and mobile application components. The platform will support separate experiences for buyers and vendors, with administrative controls for managing marketplace activities.

The project has been structured into three packages to allow Gold Link to select the level of functionality required at launch.

02

Understanding of the Project

Gold Link is designed as a digital marketplace connecting verified gold vendors with buyers. Vendors will be able to create profiles and list physical gold with information such as gold type, weight, description, images, pricing and other relevant details. Buyers will be able to browse available gold listings, review vendor information, initiate transactions and manage their purchases.

The platform is expected to support:

  • Buyer accounts
  • Vendor accounts
  • Separate buyer and vendor dashboards
  • Gold listings
  • Vendor registration information
  • Gold price display
  • Transaction management
  • Secure payment through your provider
  • KYC verification
  • Escrow functionality
  • Messaging
  • Transaction history
  • Gold holdings information
  • Administrative management

The Gold Vault feature will remain marked as Coming Soon until the required real-world asset integration is available.

The public website will primarily serve as the marketing and information platform for Gold Link, while the marketplace functionality will be delivered through the selected application/platform package.

03

Project Objectives

The objectives of the Gold Link project are to:

  • Build a professional digital presence for Gold Link
  • Create a secure marketplace for buying and selling physical gold
  • Provide separate experiences for buyers and vendors
  • Enable secure digital transactions
  • Support direct purchase and, where included, escrow transactions
  • Improve trust between buyers and sellers through verification and vendor information
  • Provide transaction tracking and history
  • Support KYC and identity verification
  • Display relevant gold market pricing information
  • Build a scalable foundation for future expansion into additional markets and features

The platform will display gold market pricing based on the agreed gold price data source and the approved pricing methodology. The displayed market reference price may differ from the final price set by individual vendors.

Mobile Applications

Android Application

The Android application will provide access to the Gold Link marketplace through supported Android devices. The Android application will include the features contained within the selected package. The Basic package includes Google Play Store submission only.

iOS Application

The iOS application is included only in the Professional and Premium packages. Apple App Store submission is also included only in these packages.

App Store review and approval timelines are controlled by Apple and are therefore outside the control of CodeLikeWyna Tech. Financial and marketplace applications may also require additional documentation, compliance information or clarification during the store review process.

Project Timeline

The estimated project duration is up to two (2) months. The timeline begins after:

  • The required initial payment has been received
  • The required branding materials have been supplied
  • Required API credentials and technical documentation have been supplied
  • Required company and compliance documents have been supplied
  • The client has approved the relevant project requirements

Development will be carried out in phases, beginning with the website and progressing into the marketplace and application components.

Client delays, unavailable third-party services, delayed approvals, missing documents or changes to the agreed scope may extend the delivery timeline.

The meeting discussions also established that the website would be developed first before proceeding into the wider application roadmap.
