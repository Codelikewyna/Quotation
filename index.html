Executive Summary

CodeLikeWyna Tech is pleased to present this proposal for the design, development, testing, and deployment of the Gold Link digital marketplace.

The Gold Link platform is intended to become a trusted marketplace where verified gold vendors and buyers can safely buy and sell physical gold through a secure digital platform. The solution will consist of:

Marketing Website Marketplace Web App Android Application iOS Application

The project will be delivered over a period of two (2) months, beginning from the date the initial payment is received and all required project materials have been supplied by the client. Development will follow a phased approach, with the website being completed first before the mobile applications — aligned with the roadmap discussed during the planning meeting.

The total investment for the complete project is ₦2,840,000 (Two Million, Eight Hundred and Forty Thousand Naira Only). The project cost will be paid in three milestones tied to specific deliverables throughout the development process.

The platform will support secure transactions through Simpay, vendor verification using Smile Identity, escrow payment functionality, buyer and vendor dashboards, transaction history, instant messaging, watchlists, and other marketplace features.

02

Understanding of the Project

Gold Link is intended to become a marketplace where verified vendors can list physical gold for sale while buyers can securely purchase gold using trusted payment methods. The platform will provide separate experiences for buyers and vendors, while allowing administrators to monitor all marketplace activity.

The first deliverable is a website that introduces the business, builds trust, and directs users to download the mobile applications. Following the completion of the website, development proceeds with the marketplace platform and the Android and iOS applications.
