Executive Summary

A proven model, rebuilt for scale

This proposal seeks approval to begin development, branding, launch, and marketing of a university Computer Based Test (CBT) learning platform, based on a business model I previously created and managed successfully.

The proposed project will replicate and improve upon the success of MyGesHub, a platform I founded in 2020 to help university students prepare for General Studies (GES) examinations through realistic CBT practice.

To avoid future branding and ownership complications, I recommend the new platform be launched under a completely new brand name, owned outright by Emperor Guild Studios.

The objective is a scalable edtech product capable of generating recurring income while helping thousands of university students improve their academic performance.

02
Background

Where this idea comes from

In 2020, I founded MyGesHub to solve a common problem: most University of Port Harcourt students preparing for GES examinations relied on PDFs, handwritten notes, and outdated study materials — methods that did not properly prepare them for the actual Computer Based Test.

I built a platform that let students practice in a real CBT environment. It quickly gained acceptance because it closely matched the exam experience while offering a large collection of practice questions, and the business went on to become financially successful.

Beyond revenue, the platform gave back to the student community — sponsoring activities, awarding scholarships, organising competitions, and rewarding outstanding students.

The business was eventually shut down after I came under pressure from a newly elected Students' Union Government (SUG) leadership, who repeatedly pressured me to join a cult confraternity group in exchange for continued promotion and support. Rather than compromise my values, I chose to discontinue the business.

Although the platform closed, the market demand never disappeared. The experience, market knowledge, customer insights, and business model remain valuable — and can now be rebuilt on a stronger, more scalable foundation under Emperor Guild Studios.
