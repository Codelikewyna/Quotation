How It Works — A Simple Example

When a customer makes a purchase — using a supermarket-style example:

A customer buys:

  • 2 bottles of Coke = ₦1,000
  • 1 bag of rice = ₦70,000
Total = ₦71,000

The software would:

  • Add the products to the customer's cart
  • Calculate the ₦71,000 total
  • Apply any discount
  • Record the customer
  • Reduce Coke stock by 2 and rice stock by 1
  • Ask how the customer wants to pay
  • Record cash, transfer, or card payment
  • Generate a receipt
  • Add the ₦71,000 sale to the business's sales records
  • Update the business reports and profit calculations

Every sale on any product Skyy Aluminium deals in will follow this same automatic flow, tailored to your actual product list and pricing structure.
